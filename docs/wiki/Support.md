# Support the Project

MillCreator is free and open source. If it is useful to you, you can buy the author a coffee: **<https://buymeacoffee.com/michalsarna>**

Money is not required. You also help by:
* reporting bugs and ideas in [Issues](https://github.com/michalsarna/MillCreator/issues),
* trying the G-code on real machines and sharing the results,
* improving translations,
* starring the project and telling others.

Thank you!
